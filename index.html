WASD or the arrow keys move and choose, and Shift runs or steadies a grid lock. Enter or Space confirms, Escape goes back, and Escape or P pauses. N opens Field Notes, J opens the Journal with its Map, Dex, Badges, Records and Look pages, M toggles music and F toggles full screen. On touch screens the D-pad moves and chooses, A confirms, B goes back, START pauses, and holding STEADY runs or steadies a lock.
